## Choices to confirm
1. **The title is cyan**, so it pops against the yellow window and echoes the slime. Lavender is the alternative.
2. **The script pill is lilac** (lilac → lavender gradient, ink script), not the Golden Hour gold.
3. The **BMW M4 card subtitle** "Goal car · 28-day plan" isn't spoken. I can drop it or change the wording.
4. **The name hit is on both "PART-TIME JOB" and "BMW M4".** The house rule allows it only on names, and both are the video's hero names.
