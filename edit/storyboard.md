# Storyboard v3 (yellow + electric blue): “sound design” reel

House style (video-motion-study skill), **yellow-led blend** (user: “blend of colours with yellow the main colour”; options in `accents/yellow_blends.jpg`). Yellow `#FFD000` / `#FFE866` for titles, pills, rings, ticks, bars and main glows. Electric blue `#2F7BFF` (pill outer glow, explainer light pool, edge flash), blue `#3D8BFF` dots and chips, light blue `#8CC4FF` in pixel clusters and heavy-word fill. Navy `#071226` / `#0A1A33` / `#163A73` for card bodies, explainer world and shadow grade. Text `#FFFBEA`. Format stays **9:16, 1080×1920**. Face box measured with U²-Net on every still: head at y ≈ 470–990. Titles go in the top zone behind the head, cards on the chest (y 1010–1380), captions in the lower third (bottom edge 270 px up).

Preview stills: `storyboard_v3_yellow_blue/F01–F14.jpg`. Labelled boards: `storyboard_v3_yellow_blue/board_A_F01-F07.jpg`, `board_B_F08-F14.jpg`. (Earlier versions are kept for comparison: v1 purple in `storyboard_v1/`, v2 yellow-only in `storyboard_v2_yellow/`.)
