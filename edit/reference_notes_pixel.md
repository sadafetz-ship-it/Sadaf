## Pixel effects (the three kinds seen)

| Name | Where seen | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixel loader → word** | R2 0.23–0.97 s ("price.") | Up to 5 rounded square "pixels" (about 60 px, gap 10) appear left → right, **one every 2 f**, each flickering between dim and bright. They hold about 8 f. Then the hero word **wipes in left → right with a horizontal smear** (motion blur) over the pixels in about 5 f while the pixels blink out. The last pixel stays as the accent **dot** after the word. About 24 f total. |
| **Pixel dissolve (caption change)** | R1 16.83–17.10 s | The outgoing caption breaks into **scattered accent squares** (about 40–50 px, on a grid) that flicker for about 6 f. One or two cells **flare into a solid glowing bar** (17.00 s), the next words appear *through* the pixels, and 2–4 leftover pixels linger under the new word for about 10 f before blinking off. |
| **Pixel grid on a cut** | R1 7.27–8.60 s | On a hard cut, a faint grid (cells about 90 px) covers the frame. A handful of cells fill with translucent accent or white (15–35 %) and flicker, fading out over about 1.5 s. |

## Other caption moves
- **Karaoke accent:** each new word types on in the **accent colour** and settles to white about 8 f later (R1 7.4–8.1 s).
- **Pill tag + cursor arrow:** a small accent pill ("bor", "Abdurahim") above or below a word, with a cursor arrow (R1 5–7 s, 17 s).
- **Highlight bar:** an accent rectangle grows under a word, about 4 f (R1 17.27–17.37 s).
- **Swoosh streak:** a white motion streak arcs in and leaves the setup line behind (R2 0.17–0.50 s).
- **Zoom-blur cut:** scale about 110 → 100 % plus a heavy blur, 4–5 f (R2 4.5 s and 5.83 s).
