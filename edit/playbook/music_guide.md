# Music guide — "The Playbook"

## 1. What type of video this is
**Short-form finance storytelling: a "secret playbook" hook followed by a mini-documentary origin story.**
It's the business-history explainer format ("how X built a trillion-dollar empire"), told by a talking head in a suit with a skyline behind.

| Measured | Value | What it means for music |
|---|---|---|
| Speech pace | **164 wpm** overall (186–190 in the middle) | Fast and dense. The music must stay out of the voice's frequency range: no busy melody or vocals under the talking. |
| Pauses | 0.5–0.8 s at 9.0, 12.5, 19.6, 24.6 and 30.2 s | Natural places for a music hit or a drop. |
| Emotional arc | intrigue → secret/mystery → story → "didn't have" ×3 → reveal → triumph | Dark and minor at first, building to an epic payoff. |
| Visual tone | navy + lime, premium UI, money | Dark cinematic / "luxury power" (the *Succession* / *Billions* world). |

**Target sound:** dark cinematic piano + strings over a half-time trap or pulse beat, minor key, **85–100 BPM** (or 140 BPM half-time). No vocals under the speech.

## 2. Where the music should move (cue map, matched to the edit)
| Time | Spoken | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.6 | "Alright, listen up" | Single low hit or piano note on "up" (1.6 s), then a soft pulse |
| 1.6–9.5 | "the exact playbook… trillion dollar empire… everyone's eyes" | Light pulse and piano; no drums yet |
| 9.5–20.3 | "most financiers… gatekeeping… hidden in plain sight… never wanted me to find" | **Darker**: drone, ticking, tension strings. Strip the beat back. |
| 20.4–21.2 | "Now, when Steve **Schwarzman**" | **Drums enter**, with a downbeat on the name (21.16 s) |
| 25.2–30.2 | "he didn't have…" ×3 | **Stop-hits**: the music cuts or hits on each "didn't have" (25.7 / 27.4 / 29.6 s) |
| 30.8–34.0 | "But he did have something far more valuable" | **Riser** building up |
| 34.0–35.2 | "a strategy so **powerful**" | **Drop**: full beat and strings, the biggest hit on "powerful" (35.16 s) |
| 35.2–39.1 | "transformed the financial world **forever**" | Hold high, a final hit on "forever" (38.0 s), then ring out under the fade |

## 5. How I'll mix it once you send a track
1. Trim and position it so the **drop lands on "a strategy" (34.0 s)** and the drums enter on "Schwarzman" (21.16 s).
2. Sit it under the voice at about −24 LUFS, with **side-chain ducking** (−6 dB while he speaks).
3. Add stop-hits on the three "didn't have" lines.
4. Re-time any animation hits to the beat if needed, then re-render (about 5 min).
