Storyboard · First Minute (Hook + Intro)

Every frame below is a design mock-up for this edit, built from Clay's own footage and screen recording. “If I Started an AI Marketing Agency In 2026, I'd Do This” · Clay Lawrence · Cut length 1:05.9 · 16 beats · 1920×1080 · 30 fps
Style (matched to the reference videos)
  • Graphics: full-frame light panels (white with a soft coloured vignette at the edges) and dark panels (black with a coloured edge glow). One accent colour, used only on the key words. Accent = Clay's brand green #0F782B.
  • Type: one clean rounded sans (Poppins) everywhere, with consistent sizes. Titles are italic. No text strokes, only soft shadows (editing notes).
  • Text animation: graphics cut in (no slides or transitions); only the text animates, with words fading in one by one in sync with the voice. Numbers type in.
  • Captions: on talking-head shots, key phrases only, with one phrase in green.
  • Teasers: a 'LATER IN THE VIDEO' pill over real footage from later in the video.
  • Proof: Clay's real screenshots, with a headline above and the key number in a green highlight label that types in.
  • Lists: white rounded card with the face-cam in a rounded-square inset overlapping its left edge.
  • Talking head: centred, eyes near the upper third, warm grade. Cuts are hidden with crop-ins (never mid-sentence) or by cutting to a graphic. No light leaks or flashy transitions.
  • Compliance: 'results vary / not guaranteed' stays on screen for every result claim. No invented charts or numbers.
