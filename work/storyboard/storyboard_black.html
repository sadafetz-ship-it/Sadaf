Every frame is a still from the actual edit. “If I Started an AI Marketing Agency In 2026, I'd Do This” · Clay Lawrence · 1:04.4 · 16 beats · 1920×1080 · 30 fps
Style (from the two 'Overall style for layouts and edit' references in the editing notes)
  • Theme: black backgrounds with Clay's green (#0F782B, brighter tint on black) as the only accent: sweeping green curves and a faint green haze in the corners. Dark rounded cards, white text, key words in green.
  • Type: Poppins everywhere, consistent sizes, italic card titles. No text strokes, only soft shadows (editing notes).
  • Animation: cards fade in empty, then words pop in one by one with the voice. Diagrams build box by box with dashed arrows drawing in. Numbers count up in place.
  • Captions: on talking-head shots, key phrases only, with the key words in green.
  • Proof: Clay's real Stripe dashboard with a slow push-in, soft blurred edges, a live count-up and a green marker. 'Results not typical or guaranteed' on every result claim; no invented numbers.
  • Teaser: 'LATER IN THIS VIDEO' over a blurred montage of his real screen recording; face-cam bottom right.
  • Cut: best take of every line, natural breaths kept, short fades on every audio cut. Jump cuts hidden by crop-ins at sentence breaks or by cutting to a graphic. No light leaks or flashy transitions.
  • Sound: dedicated mic only, cleaned gently, -14 LUFS with no volume jumps. Music ('Inspired', Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) under the voice with ducking. Real CC0 sound effects, kept subtle.
