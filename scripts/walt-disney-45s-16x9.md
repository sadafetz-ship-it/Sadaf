## Notes
- **Fact check before posting:**
  - Laugh-O-Gram Studio went bankrupt in 1923.
  - Universal owned Oswald, and in 1928 Charles Mintz took the series and most of Disney's animators.
  - Steamboat Willie premiered in November 1928. Call it "one of the first cartoons with synchronized sound", not "the first".
  - "Disney's Folly" is the commonly reported nickname for Snow White.
  - Walt Disney holds the record for most Oscars won by an individual: 22 competitive awards (26 including honorary ones).
- **Optional extra:** the story about him being fired from a newspaper for "lacking imagination" is widely repeated but poorly sourced, so I left it out.
- **Images and characters:** Mickey, Oswald and Snow White are copyrighted characters and trademarks. Steamboat Willie (1928) entered the US public domain in 2024, but later Mickey designs and Snow White did not. Use editorial or licensed clips, or your own sketches that hint at the characters without copying them.
- **16:9 layout:** subject on the right third, captions on the left third. Scale the hero words up so they still dominate the frame.
