## Notes
- **Fact check before posting:**
  - Einstein graduated in 1900, couldn't get an academic post, and joined the Swiss patent office in Bern in 1902 as a "technical expert, third class".
  - In 1905 he published the four "miracle year" papers: the photoelectric effect (light), Brownian motion (atoms), special relativity (time), and mass-energy equivalence (E = mc²).
  - The 1919 eclipse observations backed general relativity and made him famous worldwide.
  - He received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics, awarded in 1922, for the photoelectric effect, not relativity.
- **Nuance:** "proved atoms exist" is a simplification. His Brownian-motion paper gave the evidence, and Jean Perrin's experiments confirmed it. "Helped prove" is safer if you want to be precise.
- **Myth to avoid:** he did *not* fail maths in school. Don't add that line.
- **Images:** many Einstein photos are public domain, but his name and likeness are commercially licensed. Fine for editorial or educational use. Check before using them in ads.
- **16:9 layout:** subject on the right third, captions on the left third. Scale the hero words up so they still dominate the frame.
