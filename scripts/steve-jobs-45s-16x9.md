**Format:** 16:9 (1920×1080), 30 fps · **Runtime:** 45 s · **VO:** ~115 words, calm and punchy

## Style recipe (taken from the 4 reference reels)
| Element | How the references do it | What to do in 16:9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening** | A year in thin serif on cream paper ("In 1955", "[in 1974]", "Netflix in 2022"), then a push into a framed photo | Same, centred. "In 1985" in brackets, then a push into a gold picture frame |
| **Captions** | 1–3 words at a time, synced to the VO. White italic serif over footage. One **hero word** per line in heavy bold sans, often in a red or yellow box ("smashed", "her lab", "system", "billion") | Captions sit in the left third, subject in the right third. Hero word 2–3× the caption size |
| **Colour** | Desaturated or B&W photos, warm cream paper, film grain, heavy vignette. One accent: red (#C8102E-ish) and mustard yellow (#E3C21A-ish); the lime-green money in V01 | B&W Jobs, red box for bad news, yellow for wins |
| **Big numbers** | A giant serif numeral fills the frame ("40000", "1912", "Three hundred twenty five million") | "$10,000,000", "90", "$1 trillion" |
| **Objects** | Newspapers on a wood desk, a corkboard with red string, polaroids, a magnifying glass, a hand-drawn red marker circle, a globe with pins, gears, a clipboard | Wood desk + newspapers, corkboard, red marker circles |
| **Pacing** | A cut every 0.6–2 s, a quiet cream "What changed." card in the middle, a punchline in the last 3 s | Same |
| **Transitions** | Push through a frame, paper tear, ink or burn smear, glitch light streaks, hard cut to black on a logo | Use all of them. Glitch streaks on the Apple-logo hit |

## Notes
- **Fact check before posting:** Jobs left Apple in 1985. Pixar was bought in 1986 for about $10M, and Toy Story came out in 1995. Apple agreed to buy NeXT at the end of 1996 (it completed in 1997). "About 90 days from going broke" is Jobs' own later estimate, not an audited figure. Apple reached $1 trillion in 2018, after Jobs died (2011).
- **Images:** use licensed or editorial photos, or clearly stylised illustrations. Don't present AI-generated "photos" of a real person as real.
- **16:9 layout:** the references are vertical with the subject in the centre. In wide format put the subject on the right third and captions on the left third, and scale the hero words up so they still dominate the frame.
