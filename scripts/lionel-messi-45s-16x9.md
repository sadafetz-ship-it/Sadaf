## Notes
- **Fact check before posting:**
  - He was diagnosed with growth hormone deficiency at about 10–11.
  - The treatment is commonly reported as about $900–1,000 a month.
  - Barcelona's first offer was written on a napkin in December 2000, and he moved there as a 13-year-old.
  - He has 8 Ballon d'Or awards (a record) and won the World Cup in December 2022.
- **Wording:** "Too small" is how the story is usually told. The doctors diagnosed a hormone deficiency; they didn't literally say "too small", so the VO says "growth hormone deficiency" and the on-screen text is the story's hook.
- **Footage:** match footage and club and FIFA logos are copyrighted. Use licensed or editorial photos, or stylised illustrations.
